# Reply playbook

Ready answers for the replies cold emails usually get. The reply check gives this file to the model that drafts the suggested answer (with the Offer section of CLAUDE.md), and Alon approves every answer before it goes out. Keep each answer short, friendly and plain text, in Alon's voice, no signature (it is added automatically). Adapt the wording to what they actually wrote; never promise anything outside the Offer, never invent facts about their business. Add a new entry whenever a new kind of reply shows up twice.

## "How much is it?" / "What's the catch?"
Two simple options, and nothing to pay until you're happy with the site:
- Monthly: the first month is free, then $49 a month with hosting and up to 2 small edits a month included. There's a minimum of 2 paid months after the free one, then you can cancel anytime.
- Own it: $299 once. Hosting and one small edit are included for the first year, then hosting is $49 a year.
No setup fee and no contract beyond that. All the details are at neonsignsites.com/terms.

## "Is this a scam?" / "Who are you?"
Fair question. I'm Alon Katz and I run Neon Sign Sites. I build new websites for local service businesses from what's already public (your current site and your Google listing), and send the preview first so you can see it before deciding anything. You don't pay anything unless you want it live, and you can call me at (888) 444-1847 if you'd rather talk.

## "How did you get my information?"
I found your business on Google Maps and your email on your own website. I only use what's public: your site's text and photos and your Google rating. The preview isn't listed anywhere public, and if you'd like it removed, just say so and it's gone.

## "Looks good, let's do it" / "Yes"
Great! Pick a plan with the "Make it live" button at the top of your preview (link). Then I'll connect it to your domain: you can do it yourself in about 5 minutes with my step-by-step email, or I can do it for you. Your email keeps working as it does today.

## "Can you change X?" (before buying)
Of course. Tell me what you'd like (text, photos, prices, services, colors) and I'll update the preview, usually within a day, so you can see it before deciding.

## "I already have a web guy" / "We're happy with our site"
Totally understood. The preview is yours to keep looking at, no strings attached. If anything changes, you know where to find me. (Don't argue or push.)

## "Not now" / "Maybe later" / "Busy season"
No problem at all. I'll keep your preview up for a while; just reply whenever it suits you.

## "What happens to my domain / email?"
Your domain stays yours: I only point it at the new site, and your email keeps working exactly as it does now. If you ever leave, the site files are yours (Own it) or the site simply comes down at the end of the paid month (Monthly).

## "Can I see examples?"
Your preview is the best example: it's built from your own business. I'm happy to change anything on it so you can see how it would look.

## "No" / "Unsubscribe" / "Stop"
Don't answer. They go on do-not-contact.json automatically and are never emailed again.

## Angry or rude reply
Don't argue. Short and polite: "Sorry to bother you. I've removed you from my list and won't email you again." Add them to do-not-contact.json.
