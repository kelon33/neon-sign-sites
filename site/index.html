Questions

Good to know

Do I really see the site before paying?

Yes. We build your new site first and send you a private link. You pay only if you want it live. On the monthly plan, the first month is free.

What do you need from me?

Almost nothing. We start from your current website and your Google listing. If you have new photos or want different text, send them and we'll add them.

Who's behind Neon Sign Sites?

Me, Alon Katz. I'm behind every site we make, and I answer every email and call myself: write to hello@neonsignsites.com or call (888) 444-1847. Our mailing address is 6421 N. Florida Ave, Suite D-1009, Tampa, FL 33604.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. Your domain stays yours. We point it to the new site, and your email keeps working as it does today.

How long does it take to go live?

Usually a few days after you say yes, depending on how quickly we can connect your domain.

What's the difference between owning it and the monthly plan?

Own it: you pay $299 once and the site is yours. Hosting and 1 small edit a year are included for the first year, then $49 a year; more changes are $25 each. Monthly: your first month is free, then $49 a month (minimum 2 paid months), with hosting and up to 2 small edits a month included. The site stays live as long as you subscribe.

What counts as a small edit?

One change to what's already on your site: text, prices, hours, phone number, service areas, or swapping or adding up to 5 photos. We make it within 2 business days. New pages, new sections or a redesign are quoted separately, from $25.

What if I want to cancel the monthly plan?

After your free month and 2 paid months, cancel anytime. Your site comes down at the end of the paid month, or you can keep it for a one-time $299.
