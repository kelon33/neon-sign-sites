Who we are

Neon Sign Sites builds websites for local service businesses. It is run by Alon Katz. You can reach us at hello@neonsignsites.com.

Free previews

We may build a preview of a new website for your business from information that is already public: your current website and your Google listing. A preview is free and you are under no obligation. Preview pages are not listed in search engines. Ask us and we remove a preview right away; previews nobody takes are removed after 30 days.

Own it: $299 one-time

  • Your complete website, launched on your own domain.
  • Hosting and SSL included for the first year, then $49 a year.
  • Changes after launch are $25 each, or $19 a month for ongoing care.
  • You own the finished site and can move it elsewhere at any time.

Monthly: first month free, then $49 a month

  • Your complete website, launched on your own domain, with hosting, SSL and backups included.
  • Up to 2 small edits a month: text, photos, prices or hours.
  • The first month after launch is free. We take your card at signup and the first $49 charge is on day 31.
  • There is a minimum of 2 paid months after the free month ($98 in total); after that, cancel any time. If you cancel during the free month, you pay nothing and the site comes down.
  • One free month per business.
  • When you cancel, the site comes down at the end of the paid month, or you can keep it for a one-time $299.
  • You can switch to owning the site for $299 whenever you like.

Your domain and your content

Your domain stays yours; we only point it to the new site. You confirm that you have the right to use the text, photos and logo on your site. We start from what is on your current website and change anything you ask us to.

Payment

Prices are in US dollars. You pay only after you have seen your site and asked for it to go live. On the monthly plan, your card is charged from day 31 after launch.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we post the new version here. The terms in place when you paid apply to that payment.
